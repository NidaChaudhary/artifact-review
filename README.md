​🌸 About Artifact
​Artifact is a sleek, minimalist web utility designed to protect your personal privacy before sharing images online. When you snap a photo with your phone or camera, it secretly stores EXIF metadata inside the file—including your exact GPS coordinates, device timestamp, and camera model.
​Artifact instantly strips away all these hidden tracking footprints right inside your browser with zero server transmission.
​🚀 Key Features & Workflow
​🌿 100% Client-Side Processing: Your photos never leave your device. The entire sanitization runs locally in your browser.
​☕ Instant Metadata Wiping: Wipes away all hidden GPS locations, timestamps, and device signatures in a single click.
​🎮 Retro Dark Aesthetic: Styled with a clean 2D retro compass badge and a soothing dark charcoal/amber interface.
​🛡️ Zero Data Retention Guarantee: Absolute privacy assurance for every visitor.
​💻 How to Use (Step-by-Step Instructions)
​Visit the Live App: Open your browser and go to the official deployed link:
👉 Artifact Live Webpage
​Upload Your Image: Drag and drop any image (PNG, JPG, WEBP) into the interactive sanitizer dropzone or click to browse files from your device.
​Inspect the Security Report: Watch the live system verify that the GPS exposure has been successfully stripped and the canvas has been re-rendered cleanly.
​Download & Share: Save your sanitized photo instantly and share it anywhere with total peace of mind!
