"Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication." • Built with clean code, zero server uploads, and privacy in mind.

✨ What is Artifact?

Artifact is a lightweight, client-side web utility designed to protect your personal privacy before sharing images online. When you snap a photo with your phone or camera, it secretly stores EXIF metadata inside the file—including your exact GPS coordinates, device timestamp, camera model, and device fingerprint.

Artifact instantly strips away all these hidden tracking footprints right inside your browser with zero server transmission.

🚀 Key Features

Client-Side Processing: Your photos never leave your device. 100% private, zero data retention.

Instant Metadata Stripping: Instantly wipes GPS locations, camera signatures, and hidden timestamps.

Retro Dark Aesthetic: Styled with a clean, custom retro compass emblem and dark charcoal/amber interface.

Interactive Secure Review System: Submit your thoughts and rate the privacy tool with live updates.

💻 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

Visit the Live App: Open the live GitHub Pages link: Artifact Live App

Upload an Image: Drag and drop any image (PNG, JPG, WEBP) into the sanitizer dropzone or click to browse.

Inspect & Clean: Watch the live security report verify that the GPS coordinates and camera fingerprints have been completely destroyed.

Share Securely: Download your sanitized image and share it anywhere with total peace of mind!
